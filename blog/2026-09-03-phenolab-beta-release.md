We are excited to share the beta release of PhenoLab, a crop phenotyping web platform for organizing field studies, managing plot-level research assets, running reusable analysis workflows, and turning experimental data into research-ready outputs.

{/* truncate */}

This beta brings the main pieces of the PhenoLab workflow together in one browser-based workspace:

- **Project and study organization** for structuring research work across projects, studies, datasets, plots, and assets.
- **Dataset editing tools** for registering plot-level imagery, sensor data, metadata, treatments, and supporting files.
- **Reusable LgoPy analysis modules** that can be installed, searched, inspected, and combined like building blocks for phenotyping and remote-sensing workflows.
- **Analysis pipelines** for running dataset-level workflows, tracking progress, reviewing logs, and collecting generated artifacts.
- **Results and exports** that keep derived files, figures, tables, metadata, and pipeline outputs connected to the original study context.
- **Configuration and deployment documentation** for running PhenoLab locally today while keeping a clear path toward hosted deployments.

The beta also introduces the direction for PhenoLab's scientific knowledge base and agent-assisted workflows. The goal is to help research teams move from multimodal field observations to defensible findings, downstream analysis, scientific discovery, manuscripts, and grant proposals while keeping methods, provenance, and outputs organized.

This release is an important step toward a more modular, reusable, and transparent phenotyping platform. We will use this blog to share updates, release notes, tutorials, and development progress as PhenoLab continues to evolve.
